# 🏫 Digital School — Complete School Management System

**Digital School** is a modern, full-featured School Management System built with **Python & Django**. Designed for primary, secondary, and high schools, it streamlines daily administration, fee collection, family/sibling billing, attendance tracking, examinations, payroll, and official certificate generation—all from a clean, responsive dashboard.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 💰 Fee & Financial Management
* **Flexible Billing Modes:** Supports both **Single Student** and **Sibling / Family Grouped** fee collection and voucher generation.
* **Fee Collection & Vouchers:** Generate monthly vouchers, apply automatic or on-spot discounts, fines, and rolling/legacy arrears, and print thermal or A4 receipts.
* **Family Yearly Fee Reports:** Track combined sibling payments across any custom month range and year with print-ready statements.
* **Daily Collection & Monthly Financial Statements:** Real-time daily collection summaries, expense tracking with category budgets, global income ledger, and printable monthly profit/loss statements.
* **Staff Payroll:** Automated salary calculation based on attendance, bonuses, deductions, and printable payslips.

### 📋 Attendance Management
* **Daily Student & Staff Attendance:** Quick one-click marking (*All Present / All Absent*), live status counters, donut chart summary, and absence alerts.
* **Live Attendance Logs:** Instant AJAX-based filtering by student name, class, date, and user type.
* **Monthly Attendance Reports:** Class-wise attendance percentages with **Print / PDF** and **Excel (CSV)** export.

### 📝 Examinations & Results
* **Exam Scheduling & Marks Entry:** Subject-wise marks entry with dynamic grading scales, automatic pass/fail calculation, and class positions.
* **Exam Forms & Admit Cards:** Customizable **Bio Data** and **Admit Cards (Exam 1 & Exam 2)** with selectable sections, medium, and subjects—formatted to print cleanly on A4.
* **Result Cards & Exam Summaries:** Class-wise pass/fail summaries and printable student marksheets.

### 🎓 Official Certificates
* **4 Built-in Printable Certificates:**
  * School Leaving Certificate (with Book No., Page No., G.R. No., and Duplicate tracking)
  * Date of Birth (D.O.B) Certificate
  * Testimonial / Pass Certificate
  * Character Certificate

### 📚 Academics & Administration
* **Academic Hub:** Manage Study Notes, Lesson Plans, and Student Tasks by class and subject.
* **Timetable & Periods:** Weekly class/exam timetable builder with printable schedules.
* **User & Role Management:** Granular role-based permissions for Admins, Accountants, Teachers, and Parents, plus database Backup & Restore.
